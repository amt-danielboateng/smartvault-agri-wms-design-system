Project Proposal: SmartVault Agri-WMS
Intelligent Warehouse Management & Electronic Warehouse Receipt System (e-WRS)
Prepared By: Engineering Team
Target Deployment: Ghana (Ashanti, Bono, Ahafo, and Northern Agricultural Belts)
Document Version: 1.0
1. Executive Summary
In response to agricultural shifts towards national food reserves, regional commodity aggregation, and decentralized storage networks across West Africa, post-harvest infrastructure requires modern digital enablement. Sub-Saharan Africa loses between 15% and 40% of staple crop yields annually due to inadequate post-harvest handling, unmonitored silo conditions, and inefficient market linkages. Furthermore, smallholder farmers face liquidity crunches at harvest, forcing distress sales at depressed prices.
SmartVault Agri-WMS is a specialized, offline-first Warehouse Management System integrated with an Electronic Warehouse Receipt System (e-WRS) and IoT telemetry. It provides agricultural aggregators, state warehousing boards, and cooperative grain reserves with end-to-end operational visibility: from gate intake and moisture/grade verification to real-time microclimate silo monitoring and collateralized digital receipts for financial inclusion.
2. Problem Statement
Modern bulk storage facilities (handling maize, cereals, tubers, and legumes) encounter three operational bottlenecks:
Information Asymmetry & Quality Disputes: Inconsistent manual grading at intake points leads to disputes between aggregators and farmers regarding moisture content, foreign matter, and grade categorization.
Spoilage & Storage Losses: Storage silos and barns lack continuous environmental monitoring, allowing hot spots, moisture accumulation, and pest infestations to damage large batches unnoticed.
Liquidity Traps for Smallholders: Farmers cannot easily leverage stored inventory as collateral because conventional financial institutions lack verifiable, tamper-evident visibility into stored stock volumes and quality.
3. Proposed Solution Architecture
SmartVault Agri-WMS operates as an integrated three-tier platform:
Intake & Grading Module (Edge-Enabled PWA): An offline-first Progressive Web App utilized by intake officers to weigh incoming hauls, capture laboratory moisture readings, assign official grade classifications (Grade 1 to Grade 3), and print QR-coded batch tags.
IoT Telemetry & Ambient Monitoring Engine: Low-power environmental sensor nodes deployed throughout warehouse zones transmitting real-time temperature and relative humidity metrics over MQTT to prevent spoilage and trigger predictive aeration alerts.
Digital Warehouse Receipt System (e-WRS) & Collateral Registry: A cryptographically signed digital receipt engine providing farmers and partnering financial institutions with verified proof of ownership, batch quality grade, and current market valuations.

5. Core Data Model & Schema Overview
The core relational schema balances strict traceability with high transaction throughput:
CommodityType: Defines commodity taxonomy (Maize, Soya, Cowpea, Paddy Rice) and acceptable physical grading thresholds (maximum moisture percentage, foreign matter tolerance).
Warehouse & Bin: Hierarchical physical facility hierarchy with 3D capacity limits and zone designations.
IntakeConsignment: Captures gross/tare weights, moisture testing readings, foreign matter percentage, and resulting grade certification.
WarehouseReceipt: Cryptographically hashed certificate representing ownership, status (Issued, Pledged, Released, Void), and lien holder associations.
TelemetryMetric: Time-series logging of internal ambient temperature, external ambient temperature, and relative humidity.

7. Key Risk Mitigation & Operational Considerations
Intermittent Connectivity: Storage hubs often face cellular outages. The client uses service workers and IndexedDB to cache all intake actions locally, queueing them for automatic reconciliation when the network reconnects.
Dust and Harsh Environmental Conditions: Physical sensors must carry minimum IP65 ratings and operate via battery or solar arrays with sleep-cycle telemetry to endure seasonal humidity and dust.
Farmer Accessibility: Because most smallholders rely on feature phones, receipt confirmation and status checks do not require web access; automated two-way USSD/SMS flows keep producers informed in real time.
8. Conclusion & Expected Impact
SmartVault Agri-WMS transforms commodity storage facilities into transparent, data-driven supply chain hubs. By eliminating manual grading ambiguities, monitoring grain health continuously, and unlocking liquidity through e-WRS, the platform secures critical food reserves, minimizes post-harvest loss, and creates trust between farmers, aggregators, and financial institutions.
